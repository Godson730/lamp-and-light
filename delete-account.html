Delete your Lamp & Light account

Lamp & Light only has accounts for the optional Groups feature. Your Bible reading, notes and plans are stored on your phone, not in an account.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lamp & Light and tap Groups.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

Can't open the app? Ask us by email

Email jamesagyapong1234@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Lamp & Light account”. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email address and display name
  • Your messages, prayer requests and “I prayed” marks
  • Your group memberships and the list of people you blocked
  • Groups you lead, including everything posted in them

Reports you filed that are still waiting for a leader's review may be kept until reviewed, and then deleted. To remove on-device data, use Settings › Reset all data in the app, or uninstall it.
