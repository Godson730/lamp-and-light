This policy explains how the Lamp & Light app (Android) and its browser version (together, “the app”), published by Lamp & Light, handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your phone:

DataPurpose
Notes, journal entries, highlights and bookmarksSo you can return to your Bible study and fasting records
Study progress, fasting plan and fasting historyTo show your weekly lesson, fasting schedule and streak
Reminder times and settings (theme, text size, optional first name for greetings)To schedule reminders and personalise the app

This information is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no servers and no way to see it. In the browser version, the same information is kept in your browser's local storage on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. The complete Bible text is built into the app, so it does not connect to the internet to work.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
NotificationsTo show your prayer, Bible study and fasting reminders.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)Optional. Lets reminders ring at the exact minute you chose. If you don't allow it, Android may deliver reminders a few minutes late.
Run at startupSo your reminders are restored after your phone restarts.
Prevent phone from sleeping (wake lock)Briefly used to deliver a reminder on time.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup (for example, to your Google account). That backup is controlled by you and Google under your device settings; we cannot access it.

Deleting your data

Open the app, tap the settings (gear) icon and choose Reset all data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the app stored.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Scripture texts

The King James Version and World English Bible texts included in the app are in the public domain and were obtained from eBible.org. “World English Bible” is a trademark of eBible.org.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
