Privacy Policy

Lamp & Light — Bible Study Guide · Effective date: September 15, 2026

This policy explains how the Lamp & Light app (Android) and its browser version (together, “the app”), published by Lamp & Light, handles your information. We never sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Most of the app (the Bible, commentary, reading plans, study, fasting guide, reminders and audio) works entirely on your device and sends nothing to us. Only the optional Groups feature stores information online, and only if you create a groups account.

1. Information stored only on your device

DataPurpose
Notes, journal entries, highlights and bookmarksSo you can return to your Bible study and fasting records
Reading plan progress, study progress, fasting plan and historyTo show your daily reading, weekly lesson and fasting schedule
Reminder times and settings (theme, text size, voice, optional first name for greetings)To schedule reminders and personalise the app

This information stays on your device. We have no way to see it.

2. Groups (optional, requires an account)

If you create a groups account, the following is stored with Google Firebase, the online service that runs groups:

Email address and passwordNobody in the app. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.To sign you in and reset a forgotten password

Groups are private and invite-only; nothing is published publicly. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and may be stored on Google's servers outside your country. The app developer can access groups data through the Firebase console, but only uses it to keep the service running, investigate reports of abuse, or where required by law.

Google processes this data as our service provider under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

3. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Messages and prayer requests stay until you delete them, a group leader removes them, or the group is deleted.
  • Leaving a group removes your membership; your past posts stay unless you delete them or delete your account.
  • Delete your account in the app: Groups › your name › Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, messages, prayer requests, memberships and blocks, and deletes any groups you lead. You can also request deletion by email.
  • On-device data is removed with Settings › Reset all data or by uninstalling the app.

4. Read-aloud (audio Bible)

When you use “Listen”, the Bible text is spoken by your phone's or browser's own text-to-speech engine. The app sends nothing to us. Most voices work entirely on your device. If you choose a voice marked “needs internet”, your device's speech service (for example Google's) processes the text being read, under that provider's own privacy policy.

5. Sharing

When you share a verse, reading or invite, it goes to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) under that app's privacy policy. Invite links only contain a reading-plan code or group code, not personal information.

7. Backups

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's on-device data in your personal device backup (for example, to your Google account). That backup is controlled by you and Google; we cannot access it.

8. Children

Groups are only for people aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created a groups account, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Scripture and commentary

The King James Version and World English Bible texts are in the public domain and were obtained from eBible.org (“World English Bible” is a trademark of eBible.org). The Jamieson-Fausset-Brown commentary (1871) is in the public domain.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
